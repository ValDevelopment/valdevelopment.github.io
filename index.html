Clinical Trial Safety Analysis

An end-to-end clinical trial safety analysis using CDISC SDTM and ADaM datasets from a public 254-patient Alzheimer's trial. The project summarizes baseline characteristics and adverse events, then evaluates time to first adverse event across treatment groups.

Kaplan-Meier estimates and Cox proportional hazards models show a dose-response safety signal, with higher treatment doses associated with substantially greater adverse-event risk than placebo. An age-adjusted sensitivity analysis assesses the robustness of the result.

League of Legends Win Prediction & Analytics

A statistical and machine-learning analysis of more than 47,000 professional League of Legends games. The project estimates how strongly early-game metrics predict match outcomes and compares predictive relationships across seasons and competitive regions.

It includes an XGBoost win-prediction model, ARIMA and ETS forecasts of evolving meta trends, a bootstrapped Elo system for cross-region team strength, and an interactive Tableau dashboard for exploring early-game predictors.

FAERS Disproportionality Analysis: Semaglutide

A post-market drug safety analysis of semaglutide reports in the FDA FAERS Q4 2025 dataset. The project identifies adverse events reported disproportionately often for Ozempic, Wegovy, and Rybelsus among 3,279 primary-suspect case reports.

Potential safety signals are evaluated using PRR, ROR, chi-square testing, and Haldane-Anscombe correction. The results are then reviewed for MedDRA coding artifacts and compared with established and emerging findings in the GLP-1 safety literature.

OpenPowerlifting Relational Database

A normalized relational database containing 100,000 OpenPowerlifting competition results. Lifters, meets, and individual performances are stored in linked MySQL tables with primary and foreign key constraints.

The database supports SQL analysis of federation participation, performance differences, sanctioned competitions, and lift-attempt success rates using joins, conditional aggregation, common table expressions, and window functions.

Training Periodization Agent

An AI-assisted strength training program generator based on two years of self-logged squat, bench press, and deadlift data. The project identifies personalized weekly percentage-of-max patterns and uses them to define a training approach that is more intensive than standard published templates.

The Streamlit app generates new squat, bench press, and deadlift programs through an AI agent grounded in those historical training patterns, rather than relying only on generic programming advice.
